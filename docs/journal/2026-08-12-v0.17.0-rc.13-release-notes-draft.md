## Proposed prerelease text

`@hypercarrier/pi-team-bright@0.17.0-rc.13` prepares the semantic-hardening
source series after published rc.10. It keeps the Task-first Team model and the
existing public tool, schema, package-export, observation, persistence, retry,
timing, and default-behavior contracts unchanged by default.

Internally, the candidate separates Task, Team, Alert, and Coordination
responsibilities through consumer-owned ports and durable adapters. It adds
outside-in behavior characterization, deterministic lock-contention checks,
Task-hydration measurement support, and broader aggregate verification. The
Beads runner now applies one process-group deadline and records payload-free
lifecycle evidence. This is an intentional wall-clock behavior change, but it
does not establish a breaking public contract.

## Compatibility and proof limits

- Existing public tools, schemas, package exports, persisted records, filenames,
  compatibility readers, retry behavior, timing policy, and defaults remain the
  compatibility target. Do not claim a public behavior change without separate
  owner approval and evidence.
- The local deterministic evidence does not prove a real Pi process, Beads/Dolt
  contention, external writers, watcher delivery, OS scheduling, terminal
  pixels, npm publication, registry state, provenance, or TUI freeze behavior.
- The DAG-native rc.12 line is isolated. Do not merge, tag, publish, or describe
  its DAG-native Task orchestration as part of rc.13 without an explicit source
  selection decision.

The maintained semantic-hardening context is the source for these scope and
proof-limit statements: [`context`](../projects/semantic-hardening/context.md).
