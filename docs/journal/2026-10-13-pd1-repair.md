Status: implemented. Scope: PD-1 from [oracle triage](2026-10-13-oracle-triage.md).

## Invariant and repair

Prediction: if recovery publishes verified predecessor death before it replaces
the runtime record, E1 will pass even when recovery outruns the observer poll.

Admission previously overwrote the predecessor runtime before the observer could
publish its exit. The observer then correctly rejected that stale generation.
The fix keeps the observer fence. Admission holds the Membership lease through:

1. Verify the predecessor exit and publish `process: gone` through the shared
   carrier publication module.
2. Replace the runtime generation and bind the successor Session.

An append failure prevents step 2. The predecessor remains available for retry.
Polling and admission share the accepted-journal baseline and coordinate checks.
They do not use a hidden observation store or a replacement-key guess in E1.

Source commits: `d391699` adds the ordered publication path and receipt regression;
`f4529ed` extends the carrier property reference with admission replacement.
Architecture impact: **changed** inside Pi Team Bright. Admission now requires
accepted predecessor-exit evidence before runtime replacement. Existing
publication authority, journal schema, and HyperCarrier's opaque PTB topology
remain unchanged. Decision 0021 and current context state the ordering contract.
